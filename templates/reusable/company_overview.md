# Inference Group — Company Overview

## About Us

Inference Group (trading name of TCN Capital Ltd) is a UK-based AI and data consultancy founded in 2024. We specialise in helping organisations unlock the value of their data through AI strategy, data engineering, and production AI deployment.

We are a focused team of approximately 14 professionals, combining deep technical expertise with senior leadership experience from organisations including Ofcom, Lloyds Banking Group, and Accenture (via the REPL Group acquisition).

## Core Services

- **Data & AI Strategy:** Evaluating technical feasibility, prioritising use cases, and developing structured roadmaps for AI adoption
- **Data & AI Engineering:** Building robust, scalable data pipelines, breaking down data silos, implementing MLOps and AIOps frameworks
- **Programme Management:** Bringing together data engineers, data scientists, and ML/AI specialists for compliant, secure delivery
- **AI Executive Training:** Training sessions for executives and board members on AI adoption, governance, and safe use
- **Production AI Deployment:** Deploying productionised, scalable AI solutions with governance and measurable results
- **Generative AI / LLM Solutions:** RAG pipelines, chatbots, virtual assistants, NLP-powered tools

## Differentiators

- Leadership team with senior public sector experience (former Ofcom CDO, former Lloyds Group Head of Analytics)

- Co-founders of the Business AI Alliance (launched at the Houses of Parliament), giving us a voice in UK AI policy
- RELEX Solutions certified partner for supply chain AI

## Delivery Approach

We follow GDS (Government Digital Service) delivery standards: Discovery → Alpha → Beta → Live. We work in agile sprints with fortnightly show-and-tells, clear governance, and defined decision points. We prioritise knowledge transfer so clients build internal capability alongside delivered solutions.

## Values

- **Evidence-based:** Every recommendation backed by data and feasibility analysis
- **Transparent:** Clear communication, honest about what AI can and cannot do
- **Collaborative:** We work with client teams, not in isolation
- **Outcome-focused:** Measured by impact delivered, not hours billed
