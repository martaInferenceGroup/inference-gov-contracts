# Social Value — Standard Response

## Our Commitment

Inference Group is committed to delivering social value alongside every contract. As a UK-based SME and co-founder of the Business AI Alliance, we believe responsible AI adoption should benefit society broadly, not just the organisations that commission it.

## Social Value Model Themes

### Fighting Climate Change
- All delivery is digital-first, minimising travel and physical resource consumption
- Remote and hybrid working reduces carbon footprint associated with commuting and office space
- Our AI and data solutions help clients make evidence-based decisions that reduce waste and improve resource efficiency
- We commit to measuring and reporting the carbon impact of our delivery approach

### Tackling Economic Inequality
- As an SME, we actively support other small businesses in our supply chain
- We commit to using local subcontractors where specialist skills are needed
- Our knowledge transfer approach ensures client teams develop lasting capability, supporting long-term employment and skills development
- We offer competitive apprenticeship and graduate opportunities in data and AI

### Equal Opportunity
- We actively promote diversity in technology and AI roles
- Our AI governance approach includes bias testing and fairness assessment as standard deliverables
- We design accessible solutions that work for all users, following WCAG guidelines
- Our training and executive education programmes are designed to be inclusive and accessible

### Wellbeing
- Ethical AI is embedded in our delivery methodology — every AI solution includes governance, explainability, and responsible use considerations
- Through the Business AI Alliance, we contribute to UK-wide responsible AI policy
- We prioritise mental health and wellbeing in our team, with flexible working and supportive management practices

### COVID-19 Recovery / Community
- We support digital skills development through training and knowledge transfer on every engagement
- Our "Under the Inference" podcast shares AI knowledge freely with the wider community
- We commit to offering pro-bono AI advisory sessions to local charities and community organisations
