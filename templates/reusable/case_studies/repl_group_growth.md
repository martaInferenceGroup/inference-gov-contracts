# Case Study: REPL Group — Technology Consultancy Scale-Up

**Tags:** technology consulting, retail, supply chain, enterprise, growth, delivery at scale
**Value:** Grew to 600+ staff, acquired by Accenture (2021)
**Duration:** Multi-year growth journey
**Key person:** Inference Group founding team (co-founders of REPL Group)

## Context

REPL Group was a technology consultancy serving retail and supply chain clients. The founding team built the company from startup to a significant technology services business serving major UK and international retailers.

## What We Did

The Inference Group founding team, as co-founders and senior leaders of REPL Group:

- Built a technology consultancy from inception to 600+ staff
- Delivered multi-million pound transformation programmes across retail and energy sectors
- Managed complex, multi-year client engagements with enterprise organisations
- Established delivery governance and quality frameworks that scaled with the business
- Led the organisation through successful acquisition by Accenture in 2021

## Outcomes

- Grew from startup to 600+ staff with sustained profitability
- Established as a trusted partner for major retailers
- Successful exit via acquisition by Accenture, validating the business model and delivery capability
- Delivery frameworks and quality processes proven at scale

## Relevance

This experience is directly relevant to contracts involving:
- Technology consulting and digital transformation
- Retail and supply chain sectors
- Demonstrating ability to deliver at scale and manage complex programmes
- Evidence of commercial maturity and organisational stability
