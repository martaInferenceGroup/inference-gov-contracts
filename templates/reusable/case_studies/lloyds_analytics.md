# Case Study: Lloyds Banking Group — Group Analytics Transformation

**Tags:** financial services, analytics, data platform, transformation, large enterprise
**Value:** Multi-million pound programme
**Duration:** 11 years (progressive transformation)
**Key person:** Dr Richard Davis (as Group Head of Analytics)

## Context

Lloyds Banking Group, one of the UK's largest financial services organisations, needed to build and scale its analytics capability across the group to support data-driven decision-making, risk management, and customer insight.

## What We Did

Dr Richard Davis, as Group Head of Analytics at Lloyds Banking Group over 11 years, led:

- Building and scaling the group analytics function from the ground up
- Developing advanced analytics capabilities including predictive modelling and customer segmentation
- Establishing data governance and quality frameworks across the banking group
- Delivering analytics solutions that directly informed business strategy and risk management
- Leading large cross-functional teams of data scientists, analysts, and engineers

## Outcomes

- Analytics function became a core strategic capability for the group
- Data-driven insights embedded into business decision-making at all levels
- Scalable analytics infrastructure and processes established
- Demonstrated ability to deliver analytics transformation at enterprise scale

## Relevance

This experience is directly relevant to contracts involving:
- Data analytics and platform development
- Large-scale organisational transformation
- Financial services or regulated industries
- Building analytics teams and capability
- Data governance at enterprise scale
