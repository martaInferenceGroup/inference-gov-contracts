# Case Study: Ofcom — Data Strategy & AI Capability

**Tags:** public sector, regulator, data strategy, AI, governance, telecoms
**Value:** Not disclosed (internal transformation)
**Duration:** Multi-year programme
**Key person:** Dr Richard Davis (as Chief Data Officer)

## Context

Ofcom, the UK's communications regulator, needed to transform its approach to data and analytics to keep pace with rapidly evolving telecoms and broadcasting markets. The organisation required a comprehensive data strategy, modern analytical capabilities, and governance frameworks to support evidence-based regulation.

## What We Did

Dr Richard Davis, as Chief Data Officer at Ofcom, led the development and implementation of the regulator's data strategy. This included:

- Defining and executing a data strategy aligned with Ofcom's regulatory objectives
- Building internal AI and analytics capability across the organisation
- Establishing data governance frameworks suitable for a regulatory environment
- Introducing advanced analytics and AI techniques to support regulatory decision-making
- Developing the organisation's data literacy and culture

## Outcomes

- Established Ofcom as a data-driven regulator with modern analytical capabilities
- Built sustainable internal capability that continued beyond the CDO tenure
- Governance frameworks balanced innovation with regulatory rigour
- Recognised externally: Dr Davis named in DataIQ 100 and AI Implementor of the Year 2023

## Relevance

This experience is directly relevant to contracts involving:
- Public sector data strategy and AI adoption
- Regulatory or governance-heavy environments
- Organisational transformation and capability building
- Data governance and responsible AI frameworks
