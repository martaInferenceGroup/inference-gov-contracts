# Policy Document References

This file summarises the policies Inference Group holds for tender compliance. Each entry can be referenced in SQ and ITT responses.

## Equality & Diversity Policy
**Status:** In place
Inference Group is committed to equality of opportunity in employment and service delivery. We actively promote diversity in technology and AI roles and ensure our recruitment, delivery, and client-facing work is free from discrimination on any protected characteristic.

## Environmental / Sustainability Policy
**Status:** In place
We operate a digital-first delivery model that minimises environmental impact. Remote and hybrid working, paperless processes, and efficient cloud computing are standard. We measure and report carbon impact where required.

## Data Protection / GDPR Policy
**Status:** In place
All data handling follows GDPR principles: lawfulness, fairness, transparency, purpose limitation, data minimisation, accuracy, storage limitation, integrity, and confidentiality. DPIAs are conducted for any processing of personal data. All team members receive annual data protection training.

## Modern Slavery Statement
**Status:** In place
Inference Group has zero tolerance for modern slavery and human trafficking. We conduct due diligence on our supply chain and require all subcontractors to confirm compliance with the Modern Slavery Act 2015.

## Health & Safety Policy
**Status:** In place
We maintain a health and safety policy covering our office environment and remote working arrangements. Risk assessments are conducted for all work locations. As a professional services consultancy, our primary H&S considerations relate to display screen equipment, office ergonomics, and mental health/wellbeing.

## Business Continuity Plan
**Status:** In place
Our distributed team and cloud-based infrastructure provide inherent resilience. Key person risk is mitigated through documentation, knowledge sharing, and cross-training. Client data and deliverables are backed up and version-controlled.

## Responsible AI Policy
**Status:** In place
As co-founders of the Business AI Alliance, we hold ourselves to high standards of responsible AI. Our policy covers: bias assessment, explainability, transparency, human oversight, data governance, and ethical use. All AI deliverables undergo responsible AI review.
